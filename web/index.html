Panel as monitor

…


      
      
Adds a 2240×1080 virtual monitor, Virtual-RYUO, to the desktop. Windows dragged past the right edge of your main monitor appear on the panel. While it runs, the panel's stats overlay and background video are hidden; brightness still works. The panel gets a new frame only when something on the virtual monitor changes. Turning it off removes the virtual monitor, which takes up to about 10 s. Needs KDE Plasma on Wayland, krfb and an NVIDIA GPU.
